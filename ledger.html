How to read a “yes” on equivalence

A term is equivalent only if (i) tensor rank and SI units match, (ii) the source that drives it is the same operator, (iii) the isolated-system residual vanishes, and (iv) the α-power counting matches a definite QED graph class. Today only the 1st/2nd-order rows clear that bar. The 3rd-order rows are a research program: treat Γ as the unknown matrix element of Buhler’s three-state sum, then demand that κ be fixed by α^{3/2} rather than fitted to thrust.
