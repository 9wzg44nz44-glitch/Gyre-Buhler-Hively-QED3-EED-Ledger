Classically: no — if fields are truly static and continuity holds with J=0, then ∂tρ+∇·J = 0, □C has no source, TEM electrostatics alone (C=0), no SLW launch, and F_net → 0 on the craft/mount. Isolated-system story (why P_matter vs P_SLW)

If outbound SLW carries +momentum, the capacitor structure fastened to a spacecraft can show a recoiling −F_net while the residual P_matter + P_SLW stays near zero. Pure SW (E=0, C≠0) stores energy only and cannot carry this momentum story.
