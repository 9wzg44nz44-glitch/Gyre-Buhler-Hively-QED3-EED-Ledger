Can SLW launch after the supply is removed?

Classically: no, if the fields are truly static and continuity holds with J=0 — then ∂tρ+∇·J = 0, □C has no source, TEM electrostatics alone (C=0), and there is no SLW launch. Residual SLW / force after isolation requires a continuity remainder: slow leakage/tunneling, bound-charge rearrangement on asymmetric faces, or a mapped α^{3/2} vacuum-momentum story — all HYP. Dan’s tunneling suggestion is treated as an open hypothesis, not a proof.

What this toy shows / does not show

  • Shows: RC-like charge build, hold with supply connected, and an isolated bound-charge regime with optional slow leak λ.
  • Shows: When ∂tρ ≠ 0 (charging), classical continuity can source □C even in CED-adjacent bookkeeping — the least-speculative educational SLW participation.
  • Does not show: a derivation of Exodus thrust, closed Buhler amplitudes, or a proof that tunneling produces net force.
  • Does not show: laboratory μN measurements — F_net and SLW power are educational scales on a 1-D canvas.

Warm skepticism

Buhler’s public talks describe continuing thrust with asymmetric capacitors; the math is not closed. Mapping that story onto Hively’s □C and g_SLW = ε₀ C E is a research instrument, not an identity. Treat tunneling / leakage λ and coupling Γ as knobs that make the hypothesis visible — dialing them up does not prove the hypothesis. Prefer the denser bookkeeping on the full ledger when you want term-by-term residuals.
